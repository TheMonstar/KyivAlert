Земельні розбіжності

Public land parcels whose registered legal purpose contradicts the park, playground, forest or school that OpenStreetMap maps on the same ground.

These are data inconsistencies requiring review, not evidence of wrongdoing. OSM boundaries are crowd-sourced and cadastral geometry is generalised in vector tiles, so every hit needs checking against the source registry.

How this was built, and what it can get wrong

Sources. Parcel boundaries and registry attributes from the State Land Cadastre via kadastrova-karta.com vector tiles, read across … tiles covering Kyiv. Public places from OpenStreetMap: … polygons in nine classes, clipped to the city boundary.

Selection. Of … parcels mapped across the Kyiv area, privately-owned ones are set aside as the expected background; … in communal, state or unstated ownership fall inside the city boundary and are examined. A parcel is flagged only on positive evidence of a contradiction — either a development purpose registered on it (housing, retail, offices), or a development land category on open green space. A blank registry field is missing data, not a contradiction, and never flags.

Geometry. Overlaps are computed in UTM zone 36N and must exceed both 100 m² and 10% of the parcel. Below that, the two datasets' independent digitising produces slivers along every shared edge — that floor removes about two thirds of raw intersections. Parcel areas reconstructed from the tiles agree with the cadastre's own declared areas to within ±0.6%.

Known limits. OSM draws a university or hospital as a single polygon over the whole campus, so parcels inside it all read as "inside a school" — and a student dormitory is lawfully registered as an apartment building. Treat the education and health hits as needing building-level checking. An OSM park tag may also predate a lawful rezoning. The map shows disagreement between two records, which is a starting point for enquiry, not a conclusion.
